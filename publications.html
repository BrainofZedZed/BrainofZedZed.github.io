Memory consolidation · bioRxiv 2025

How new neurons join a lasting memory.

Learning-activated prefrontal neurons direct the gradual recruitment of a projection population into the memory trace. These neurons, which project to the temporal association area (TEa), strengthen their encoding of memory-related behavior over weeks and become necessary for remote recall. Whole-brain mapping revealed that the same neurons send prominent branches to the claustrum; both targets increase tone-evoked responses during consolidation with distinct dynamics, and inhibiting the two branches has opposing effects on remote recall.

Directed cell-type recruitment during consolidation of a remote memory

Zeidler, Z., Yu, A. L., Seong, M. P., Hundley, R., & DeNardo, L. A.

Dopamine & learning

Learning actions that prevent threat.

Dopaminergic input to the prefrontal cortex is required for rapid avoidance learning, but not for recalling an established strategy. Dopamine signals increased with aversive outcomes and decreased with successful avoidance; these dynamics differed when animals experienced matched outcomes without behavioral control.

Dopaminergic projections to the prefrontal cortex are critical for rapid threat avoidance learning

Zeidler, Z., Fernandez Gomez, M., Gupta, T. A., Shari, M., Wilke, S. A., & DeNardo, L. A. Current Biology 35, 4259–4269 (2025).
