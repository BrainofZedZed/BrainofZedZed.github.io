Featured research

Memories mature. Their circuits change.

Zeidler et al. · bioRxiv, 2025 · Revised research summary

Recruiting new neurons into a lasting memory

Learning-activated neurons recruit a prefrontal projection population into a lasting memory. These neurons strengthen their encoding of memory-related behavior and send branches to temporal association cortex and claustrum. Inhibiting the two branches has opposing effects on remote recall, revealing how one population supports distinct functions across a distributed memory network.

Experimental & computational approach

Linking cells, circuits, and behavior.

Track neural activity

Longitudinal calcium imaging follows identified neurons over time; fiber photometry measures population and neuromodulatory signals.

Identify & test pathways

Activity-dependent labeling, whole-brain clearing and imaging, and targeted circuit manipulations connect anatomy to function.

Analyze population activity

CEBRA-based low-dimensional embeddings and population decoding reveal how neuronal activity represents experience and behavior.

Build accessible tools

Open-source software and experimental hardware support reproducible behavioral measurement.
